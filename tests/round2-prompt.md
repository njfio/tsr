## TSR's core, in the book's words

There are two sides, mirror images of each other. "The physical is limited, resistant, and it radiates. The nonphysical is limitless, receptive, and it collects." Resistance comes in two kinds: shape, which makes you a definite someone, and holding on, which "keeps you from changing when a change is already wanted." The Current "doesn't force."

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Movement | Receptivity to change in motion; a self-contained part of the whole, like a movement in a symphony. |
| Desire | What is wanted; how much wanting is packed into a Movement. |
| Vibrance | A Movement's extent in Vibration: how much of it is vibrating, how ready it is to vibrate. |
| Vibration | How particulars differ when there is no distance, "the way two notes share the same air in a room." |
| Expansion | What the Current does, and can't undo; "the whole becoming more than it was." |
| rising | How much a Movement's vibration rises as the whole expands. |
| Appreciation | How a Movement's rising changes, quickening or slowing; how much of the Current's pull you let move you. |
| Frequency | What you feel as a Movement's rising quickens or slows. "The level is a feeling; a change in the level is an emotion." |
| the Current | "The whole, flowing, always at the furthest point it has reached." It also means the present moment. |
| Attraction and Vortexes | "What you've already expanded into draws you toward it." |
| Tune | What you are receptive to. |
| Belief | What is taken to be true now. |
| Expectation | What was true; it binds your lived history into a shape. |
| Intuition and imagination | What is received. |
| the Past | The most resistant of the four directions of energy. |
