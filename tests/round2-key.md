## Scoring

For each relation: the T rating minus the average of D and W. R9 and R10 are controls taken from the book: if a judge doesn't rate their T well above D and W, that judge isn't discriminating and its other scores don't count.

If T beats both decoys on most of R1 to R8 across judges, the rule's reflections fit TSR's meanings better than chance wording; if not, the mirror only works where the author placed it.
